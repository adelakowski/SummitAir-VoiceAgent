# High-Priority Empathy Script — Vulnerable Residents

Use when there is no heat in winter or no AC in summer **and** the household includes elderly residents, medical needs, or other vulnerability.

1. Lead with genuine empathy: acknowledge how serious and stressful the situation is.
2. Reassure them you are treating this as a priority — not a standard queue.
3. Bypass the full standard booking questionnaire; collect only what is needed for an emergency dispatch (location/zone and callback number).
4. Call `flag_priority`, then offer the next available emergency slot.
5. Keep turns short and calm; ask one question at a time while you secure help.
