# Life-Safety Script — Gas Smell / Carbon Monoxide

Use this script immediately when the caller reports a gas smell, suspected gas leak, or carbon monoxide concern. Safety first — do not ask for booking details, address for scheduling, or appointment preferences before delivering these instructions.

1. Stay calm and clear. Tell the caller this may be a life-safety emergency.
2. Instruct them to **evacuate** the building right away — get everyone outside, including pets if safe to do so quickly. Do not turn lights or appliances on or off; do not use phones inside near the suspected leak if they can step outside first.
3. Once safely outside, tell them to call **911** and/or their gas company emergency line immediately.
4. Do not continue with scheduling, name collection, or availability questions until they confirm they are safe and emergency services have been contacted (or they explicitly refuse and you escalate to a human dispatcher).
5. If they remain on the line after evacuating and calling for help, offer to escalate to an emergency human dispatcher and end the booking flow.
